I teach Hindi-Urdu language at all levels and courses on the literature, religion and history of South Asia at the Australian National University. My language teaching treats Hindi as a gateway to the wider world of South Asian culture, from its literary and devotional traditions to film, music and contemporary media, and our classrooms bring together heritage and non-heritage learners studying both in person and online.

#### Programs I convene

- [Hindi Language Major](https://programsandcourses.anu.edu.au/major/HIND-MAJ) and [Minor](https://programsandcourses.anu.edu.au/minor/HIND-MIN)
- [South Asian Studies Major](https://programsandcourses.anu.edu.au/major/SASS-MAJ)
- Tibetan Language Minor
