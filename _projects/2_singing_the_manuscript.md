*Singing the Manuscript: A Multilingual Digital Infrastructure for South Asian Text and Performance Traditions* is a collaborative project with Dr Sabeena Shaikh, funded through the ANU College of Asia and the Pacific's Asia Pacific Innovation Program (APIP).

Many South Asian literary works survive both as manuscripts and as living performance traditions, yet these are usually studied separately. Textual scholars rarely work with performance materials in philological depth, while studies of performance seldom engage closely with written sources or with the multilingual settings in which these works circulate. The project brings these approaches together, developing methods and a digital framework that place texts and recordings of performance side by side, in conversation with each other.

The project builds on the digitisation and digital repatriation work of [ANUBhasha](https://anubhasha.org).
