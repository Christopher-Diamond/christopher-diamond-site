This blog is a place for shorter writing that sits between my published research and the classroom.

Most of my work concerns the literature, music and manuscript cultures of premodern North and East India, and especially Mithila and the songs of Vidyāpati. A great deal of that work happens long before anything reaches print: reading a difficult folio, tracing how a song moved between Mithila, Nepal and Bengal, or puzzling over a scribe's choices. Some of these small discoveries deserve to be shared on their own, and this is where they will go.

I expect to write about:

- **Manuscripts and sources:** individual songs, folios and texts, and what they reveal.
- **Digital work:** editing, digitisation and the digital repatriation of South Asian texts, including work with [ANUBhasha](https://anubhasha.org).
- **Teaching:** Hindi-Urdu and South Asian Studies, and what students make of premodern material.
- **Public writing:** background and further reading for essays published elsewhere.

Posts will be occasional rather than regular. Thank you for reading.
