ASIA2275 The Pleasures and Powers of Stories in South & Southeast Asia

An intensive course on how stories shape identity, religion, gender and place across South and Southeast Asia.

Instructor: Christopher L. Diamond

Term: Winter Session

A two-week intensive on narrative traditions of South and Southeast Asia, from folktales, myths and epics to oral histories, film and online media. Units include the Mahābhārata and the many Rāmāyaṇa traditions of South and Southeast Asia. Students develop their own research projects on a narrative tradition.

Most recently convened by me in winter session 2024. For current offerings, requirements and enrolment, see the official course page at ANU.
