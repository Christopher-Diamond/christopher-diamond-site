Students learn to talk about abilities, obligations and experiences and to express opinions on a range of topics. The course uses audio-visual material from India and introduces the registers of Hindi, from formal and informal speech to Sanskritised, Persianised and Hinglish vocabulary.

Most recently convened by me in Semester 1 2026. For current offerings, requirements and enrolment, see the [official course page at ANU](https://programsandcourses.anu.edu.au/course/HIND2300).
