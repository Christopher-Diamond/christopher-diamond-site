A two-week intensive on narrative traditions of South and Southeast Asia, from folktales, myths and epics to oral histories, film and online media. Units include the Mahābhārata and the many Rāmāyaṇa traditions of South and Southeast Asia. Students develop their own research projects on a narrative tradition.

Most recently convened by me in winter session 2024. For current offerings, requirements and enrolment, see the [official course page at ANU](https://programsandcourses.anu.edu.au/course/ASIA2275).
