The first course in the ANU Hindi-Urdu sequence. Students learn to read and write Devanagari, build a core vocabulary, and communicate in everyday situations, with attention throughout to the cultural contexts in which Hindi is used. Taught in person at ANU, with online students joining through ANU Online and Open Universities Australia.

Most recently convened by me in Semester 1 2025. For current offerings, requirements and enrolment, see the [official course page at ANU](https://programsandcourses.anu.edu.au/course/HIND1100).
