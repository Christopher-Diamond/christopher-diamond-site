Research Interests

Literature: Vidyāpati and Maithili lyric, bhakti poetry, eastern Indian epics and Purāṇas
Texts: Manuscript cultures, textual criticism and translation, digital philology and repatriation
History: Mithila, courts and kingship in Nepal and Bihar, Bengal and the Hindi heartland
Performance: Song and music, drama and the Maithili nāṭaka tradition
